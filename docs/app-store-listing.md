## 2. Description (4000 characters)

Evergreen: it describes the app, not the release.

### English (en-US) (1751)

```
Orthodox Calendar is a quiet, ad-free companion for the Church year. Open it on any day and you have the saints commemorated that day, the appointed readings in full, the fasting rule, and the lives of the saints behind the names.

Each day is worked out from the tradition you choose, rather than one calendar translated three ways. The Serbian calendar keeps the Serbian Church's fast, the Russian the Russian Church's, English ROCOR's, and English (New Calendar) the OCA's — day by day, including the days a feast lifts the fast.

FEATURES

• Saints and feasts for every day, with the great feasts marked
• The epistle and gospel in full, each named by the service it belongs to
• Fasting for the day, with a colour legend and the season's dates
• Lives of the saints, paired with the commemoration they belong to
• Month list and month grid — a day at a time, or a whole month at a glance
• Search every commemoration by name, in Cyrillic or Latin
• Jump to any date, or tap Today to come back
• Add a feast to your device calendar as a reminder
• Share the day's commemorations
• Light and dark themes, following the system or set by hand

CALENDARS AND LANGUAGES

• Serbian — Old (Julian) Calendar
• Russian — Old Calendar
• English — Old Calendar
• English — New (Revised Julian) Calendar

Switch whenever you like: the calendar, the readings and the fasting all follow.

OFFLINE

2025 to 2030 are built into the app and need no connection at all. Any other year from 2024 to 2099 downloads once and then stays on your device.

PRIVACY

No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. The app touches your device calendar only when you ask it to add a reminder.

May the lives of the saints and the rhythm of the Church year be a daily blessing.
```

## 3. What's New in This Version (4000 characters)

Written for someone coming from 1.2.3 — the version actually in the store —
rather than from 1.4.3, which never shipped.

### English (en-US) (1806)

```
All years 2024–2099
Browse the whole liturgical calendar, far back or far ahead. 2025–2030 are built into the app and work with no connection; any other year downloads once and stays on your device.

Fasting seasons at a glance
Great Lent, the Apostles', Dormition and Nativity fasts now appear as a banner with their dates and the day within them.

Scripture readings
Missing and truncated readings repaired across all three languages. English readings now use the King James Version with the Brenton Septuagint for the Old Testament, and the World English Bible is selectable for the New.

Saint biographies
Rebuilt from their original sources and matched to the saint they belong to — many were previously shown under the wrong commemoration.

English (New Calendar)
Corrected: feasts and fasting periods now fall on their proper Revised Julian dates. Christmas is no longer inside the Nativity Fast, and each great feast appears once rather than twice.

Fasting
Each calendar now follows its own church's fasting rules, day by day: the Serbian calendar the Serbian Church's, the Russian the Russian Church's, English ROCOR's, and English (New Calendar) the OCA's.

Saints in every year
Memorial Saturdays, the Sundays before and after the great feasts and other commemorations that move now fall on the right day in every year, not only this one. In leap years the saints from February 29 to mid-March appear on their own days.

Also fixed
• Reminders saved for the correct day, with a clear message if one can't be added
• Month arrows no longer step outside the available years
• A year that can't be loaded says so, instead of blaming your connection
• The fasting banner no longer shows a fast that has ended or not yet begun
• Empty commemoration cards removed
• Faster launch and snappier haptics
```

## 4. Keywords — Russian (100 characters)

Comma-separated, no spaces: a space costs a character. The App Store already
indexes the locale's app name and subtitle, so a word used there is wasted here —
which is why there are two versions.

**A — the Russian locale keeps the English name “Orthodox Church Calendar”** (93)

```
православный,календарь,церковный,святцы,месяцеслов,пост,жития,святые,праздники,чтения,именины
```

**B — the Russian locale is named “Православный календарь”** (94)

```
церковный,святцы,месяцеслов,пост,жития,святые,праздники,чтения,именины,юлианский,пасха,апостол
```

Both drop plurals that duplicate a singular already present, and leave out words
Apple matches anyway from the description. `святцы` and `месяцеслов` are the
words a Russian speaker actually searches for a saints' calendar with; `именины`
catches name-day searches, which this app answers well.

The English keyword field is untouched — say the word and it gets the same
treatment.
